A payment that remembers
the seller’s promise.

Before an agent pays, the seller signs what it will deliver. Korp binds that promise to the payment authorization. A later signed result can reveal a specific broken promise while keeping unrelated values private.

The proposed mechanism

One payment. A verifiable promise.

  1. The seller signs an exact requirement.
  2. The payment nonce commits to those signed terms.
  3. The seller signs a commitment to its result.
  4. A verifier checks one disclosed contradiction.

Correction: payment-nonce commitments already appear in Roundhouse KYA, Warrant and VIC. Our earlier search was incomplete. This demo remains a synthetic signature experiment; it does not settle a payment.

Existing receipts, signatures, nonce commitments and selective disclosure are prior art. The complete composition is not established as unique. See the updated protocol comparison.
