Precise scope. Visible limits.

        

          What is being researched: a compiler from signed
          Permit2 allowance batches to a maximum-withdrawal witness, accounting
          for atomic conflicts, sequential regrants and unreachable dependency
          cycles.
        

        

          Assumptions: finite allowances, standard Permit2
          behavior, fixed timestamp and declared asset weights, enough
          replenished funds and token-to-Permit2 approval, and spenders that can
          exercise all represented permissions. Token fees, rebases, new
          signatures, changing prices and chain reorganizations are outside this
          model. Unknown or unsupported inputs are rejected.
        

        

          Originality: nonce rules, cryptographic signatures,
          exhaustive search and permission revocation already exist. We have not
          established that this particular tool is a first-ever invention. The
          research record lists close implementations and rejected claims.
